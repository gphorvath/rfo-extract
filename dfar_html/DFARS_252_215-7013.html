252.215-7013 Supplies and Services Provided by Nontraditional Defense Contractors.

As prescribed in 215.110-70(f), use the following provision: SUPPLIES AND SERVICES PROVIDED BY NONTRADITIONAL DEFENSE CONTRACTORS (JUL 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0048)

(a) Offerors are advised that in accordance with 10 U.S.C. 3457, supplies and services provided by a nontraditional defense contractor, as defined in Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) 202.101, may be treated as commercial products or commercial services. The decision to apply commercial product or commercial service procedures to the procurement of supplies and services from a nontraditional defense contractor does not require a commercial product or commercial service determination and does not mean the supplies or services are commercial.

(b) In accordance with section 1826(8) and (9) of the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2026, unless waived, nontraditional defense contractors are exempt from the following cost and pricing requirements:

(1) Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 15.403 and DFARS 215.403-3.

(2) DFARS 215.105-5, Make-or-buy decisions.

(3) DFARS 215.304-70, Voluntary disclosure of defective certified cost or pricing data after award, unless the exemption from submission of certified cost or pricing data is waived.

(4) DFARS 215.405-2, Make-or-buy programs.

(5) DFARS 215.405-3, Forward pricing rate agreements.

(6) DFARS 215.405-70, Should-cost review.

(End of provision)
