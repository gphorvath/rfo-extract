252.223-7994 Prohibition of Procurement of Certain Items Containing Perfluorooctane Sulfonate or Perfluorooctanoic Acid.

As prescribed in 223.7404(b), use the following clause: PROHIBITION OF PROCUREMENT OF CERTAIN ITEMS CONTAINING PERFLUOROOCTANE SULFONATE OR PERFLUOROOCTANOIC ACID (AUG 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0018)

(a) Prohibition. The Contractor must not provide or use under this contract any of the following items that contain perfluorooctane sulfonate (PFOS) or perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA):

(1) Nonstick cookware or cooking utensils for use in galleys or dining facilities.

(2) Upholstered furniture, carpets, and rugs that have been treated with stain-resistant coatings.

(b) Subcontracts. The Contractor must include the substance of this clause, including this paragraph (b), in all subcontracts, including subcontracts for commercial products and commercial services, relating to the items listed in paragraph (a) of this clause.

(End of clause)
