SUBJECT: Class Deviation—Revolutionary Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Overhaul Part 19, Defense FAR Supplement (DFARS) Part 219, Revision 1

This class deviation revises and supersedes Class Deviation 2026-O0037, issued on January 21, 2026, and supersedes Class Deviation 2024-O0002, Revision 1, The Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business Program, issued on March 14, 2024. Effective immediately, contracting officers must use—

  • FAR part 19, Small Business, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page at https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-19, in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 (https://www.ecfr.gov);
  • The attached revised DFARS part 219, Small Business, and Appendix I, Policy and Procedures for the DoD Mentor-Protégé Program, in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 2; and
  • The attached revised DFARS Procedures, Guidance, and Information (PGI) 219, Small Business, in lieu of the PGI text published at https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/dfarspgi/current/index.html.

This revised class deviation implements the following:

  • Section 2 of E.O. 14275, Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement, which establishes the policy that the FAR “should only contain provisions required by statute or essential to sound procurement, and any FAR provisions that do not advance these objectives should be removed.
  • Section 4(a) of E.O. 14265, Modernizing Defense Acquisitions and Spurring Innovation in the Defense Industrial Base which requires the Secretary of War to eliminate or revise any unnecessary supplemental regulations or any other internal guidance, such as relevant parts of the Financial Management Regulation and Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement.
  • The Office of Management and Budget memorandum, M-25-26 issued on May 2, 2025, titled, Overhauling the Federal Acquisition Regulation, which provided additional guidance to federal agencies regarding the FAR overhaul.
  • Use of the new Subcontracting Plan Reporting system in SAM.gov in lieu of the Electronic Subcontracting Reporting System, which was decommissioned on February 20, 2026. The deviation clause at 252.219-7996, Small Business Subcontracting Plan (DoD Contracts), and its Alternates I and II, and the deviation clause at 252.219-7997, Small Business Subcontracting Plan (Test Program), are revised to reflect implementation of the new reporting system, along with PGI text at 219.302-2 and 253.219-70.
  • Verification of the eligibility of concerns for awards made under the Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business Program as directed at DFARS 219.106-70(a) and (b) in lieu of the procedures at FAR 19.106-1(b) and (c)(2). The alternate procedures are necessary until SAM.gov aligns with FAR procedures.
  • Correction of the threshold at DFARS 219.208-2 from $150 million to $100 million.
  • Revisions to section and paragraph organization and numbering; revisions to cited statutory authority titles; spelling corrections for “protégé;” arrangement of provisions and clauses in sequential order; and other revisions to streamline and simplify text.

This class deviation remains in effect until rescinded or incorporated into the FAR, DFARS, and DFARS PGI. Address inquiries regarding this class deviation to osd.pentagon.ousd-a-s.mbx.dfars@mail.mil.
