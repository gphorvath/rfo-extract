212.102-70 Restricting competition.

(a) See PGI 212.102-70 for guidance on promoting competition.

(b) For non-competitive follow-on acquisitions of supplies or services previously awarded on a non-competitive basis, include the additional documentation required by 206.104-71 and follow the procedures at PGI 206.104-71.

(c) See 206.104-71 for the approval thresholds.
