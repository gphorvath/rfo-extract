240.7008-3 Solicitation provision and contract clause.

(a) Insert the provision at 252.240-7993, Prohibition on the Procurement of Foreign-Made Unmanned Aircraft Systems—Representation, in solicitations that include the clause at 252.240-7994.

(b) Insert the clause at 252.240-7994, Prohibition on the Procurement of Foreign-Made Unmanned Aircraft Systems, in solicitations and contracts, including those for commercial products or commercial services unless—

(1) The acquisition is for—

(i) Counter-unmanned aircraft system surrogate testing and training; or

(ii) Intelligence, electronic warfare, and information warfare operations, testing, analysis, and training; or

(2) The Secretary of Defense has granted a waiver in accordance with section 848 of the NDAA for FY 2020 and section 817 of the NDAA for FY 2023.
