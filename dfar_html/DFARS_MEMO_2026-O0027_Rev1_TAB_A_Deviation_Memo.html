SUBJECT: Class Deviation—Revolutionary Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Overhaul Part 17, Defense FAR Supplement (DFARS) Part 217, Revision 1

Effective immediately, this class deviation revises and supersedes Class Deviation 2026-O0027, issued on December 18, 2025. This revision is necessary to implement section 814 of the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 (Pub. L. 119-60, 10 U.S.C. 3374(a)). This revision also implements new undefinitized contract action (UCA) reporting procedures and removes the requirement for submission of Consolidated UCA Management Plans.

Contracting officers must use—

  • The revised FAR part 17, Special Contracting Methods, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul web page at https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-17 in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 (https://www.ecfr.gov);
  • The attached revised DFARS part 217, Special Contracting Methods, in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 2; and
  • The attached revised DFARS Procedures, Guidance, and Information (PGI) 217, Special Contracting Methods, in lieu of the PGI text published on the Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy web page at https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/dfarspgi/current/index.html.

This revised class deviation implements the following:

  • Section 2 of Executive Order (E.O.) 14275, Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement, which establishes the policy that the FAR “should only contain provisions required by statute or essential to sound procurement, and any FAR provisions that do not advance these objectives should be removed.”
  • Section 4(a) of E.O. 14265, Modernizing Defense Acquisitions and Spurring Innovation in the Defense Industrial Base which requires the Secretary of War to eliminate or revise any unnecessary supplemental regulations or any other internal guidance, such as relevant parts of the Financial Management Regulation and DFARS.
  • The Office of Management and Budget memorandum M-25-26 issued on May 2, 2025, titled, Overhauling the Federal Acquisition Regulation, which provided additional guidance to Federal agencies regarding the FAR overhaul.
  • Section 814 of the NDAA for FY 2026 in DFARS 217.7404-6. Section 814 amended 10 U.S.C. 3374(a) to change how the Government calculates allowable profit on UCAs by adding two new specific categories of increased cost risk for profit determinations.
  • Revisions to reporting procedures for certain UCAs and unpriced change orders (UCOs) at DFARS 217.7405 and PGI 217.7405. Departments and agencies must use the UCA Tool module in the PARROT application in the Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment for reporting UCAs and UCOs and for preparation of Consolidated UCA Management Reports.
  • Removal of the requirement to submit Consolidated UCA Management Plans. The management plans are for internal department or agency use and do not require submission to Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy.
  • Revisions to cross-references, cited statutes, and paragraph numbering and organization.

This class deviation remains in effect until rescinded or incorporated into the FAR, DFARS, and DFARS PGI. Address inquiries regarding this class deviation to osd.pentagon.ousd-a-s.mbx.dfars@mail.mil.

John M. Tenaglia Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy
