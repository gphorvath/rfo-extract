219.108-3 Selecting acquisitions for the 8(a) Program.

Follow the procedures at PGI 219.108-3 when selecting acquisitions for the 8(a) Program. See DoD Class Deviation 2014-O0007, Prohibition on the Use of the 8(a) Business Development Program for Acquisition of Military Simulation and Military Simulation Training, issued on March 14, 2014. The class deviation prohibits contracting officers from using FAR 19.108, Contracting with the Small Business Administration (The 8(a) Program), to award prime contracts for military simulation and military simulation training.
