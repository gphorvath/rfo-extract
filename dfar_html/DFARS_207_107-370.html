207.107-370 Notifications.

When a proposed acquisition is funded entirely using DoD funds and potentially involves bundling—

(a) Publish in the Governmentwide point of entry (https://www.sam.gov) a notification of the intent to bundle the requirement at least 30 days prior to the release of a solicitation or 30 days prior to placing an order without a solicitation; and

(b) Include in the notification a brief description of measurably substantial benefits if they are expected to be derived as a result of bundling (see FAR 7.107).
