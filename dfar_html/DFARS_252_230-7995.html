252.230-7995 Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards. (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

As prescribed at 230.205-70(a), use the following clause: ALTERNATE A, COST ACCOUNTING STANDARDS (JUL 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

(a) Substitute the thresholds for full and modified Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) coverage at Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) 230.201-70(a)(1) and (a)(2), respectively, for the thresholds in 48 CFR 9903.201-2 and 48 CFR 9903.202-1, wherever they appear in the clause at Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 52.230-2, Cost Accounting Standards.

(b) Substitute “DFARS 230.205-70” for the reference to FAR 30.205 in paragraph (d) of the clause at FAR 52.230-2.

(c) Substitute “DFARS 230.205-70(b)(1)” for the reference to FAR 30.205(b) in paragraph (d) of the clause at FAR 52.230-2.

(End of clause)
