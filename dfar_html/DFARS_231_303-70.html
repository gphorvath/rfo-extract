231.303-70 Requirements.

(a)(1) Section 841 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1994 (Pub. L. 103-160) prohibits placing limitations on the amount that may be reimbursed for otherwise allowable indirect costs on contracts with institutions of higher education awarded on or after November 30, 1993, unless that same limitation applies uniformly to all other organizations performing similar work under DoD contracts.

(2) DoD contracts awarded on or after November 30, 1993, to institutions of higher education are exempt from the 26 percent indirect cost limitation imposed by OMB Uniform Guidance at 2 CFR part 200, subpart E and appendix III, because the same limitation does not apply uniformly to other organizations performing similar work.

(b) The administrative contracting officer can waive the prohibition in paragraph (a)(1) of this section if the institution of higher education requests a waiver to simplify its overall management of cost reimbursements under DoD contracts.

(c) Under 10 U.S.C. 4652, the costs cited in 231.205-22(a) are unallowable.
