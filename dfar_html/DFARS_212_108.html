212.108 Contract financing.

A payment for consumption-based solution services is not considered an advance payment and not subject to security per 10 U.S.C. 3803 when the services—

(a) Are offered on terms and conditions similar to those available to the public; and

(b) Consist of any combination of hardware, equipment, software, labor, or services that provide a seamless capability, can be metered and billed based on actual usage, and are priced in predetermined fixed-price units.
