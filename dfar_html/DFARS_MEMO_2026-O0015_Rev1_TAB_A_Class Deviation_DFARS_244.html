SUBJECT: Class Deviation—Revolutionary Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) Overhaul Part 44, Defense FAR Supplement (DFARS) Part 244, Revision 1

Effective immediately, this class deviation revises and supersedes Class Deviation 2026-O0015, issued on December 18, 2025. This revision implements section 1826 of the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2026 (Pub. L. 119-60) and updates cross-references to text relocated from part 239 to newly added part 240 and to a deviation clause. Contracting officers must use—

  • FAR part 44, Subcontracting Policies and Procedures, published on the Revolutionary FAR Overhaul (RFO) web page at https://www.acquisition.gov/far-overhaul/far-part-deviation-guide/far-overhaul-part-44 in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 1 (https://www.ecfr.gov).
  • The attached revised DFARS part 244, Subcontracting Policies and Procedures, in lieu of the text codified at 48 CFR chapter 2; and
  • The attached revised DFARS Procedures, Guidance, and Information (PGI) 244, Subcontracting Policies and Procedures, in lieu of the PGI text published on the Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy web page at https://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/dfarspgi/current/index.html.

This revised class deviation implements the following:

  • Section 2 of E.O. 14275, Restoring Common Sense to Federal Procurement, which establishes the policy that the FAR “should only contain provisions required by statute or essential to sound procurement, and any FAR provisions that do not advance these objectives should be removed.
  • Section 4(a) of E.O. 14265, Modernizing Defense Acquisitions and Spurring Innovation in the Defense Industrial Base which requires the Secretary of War to eliminate or revise any unnecessary supplemental regulations or any other internal guidance, such as relevant parts of the Financial Management Regulation and Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement.
  • The Office of Management and Budget memorandum, M-25-26 issued on May 2, 2025, titled, Overhauling the Federal Acquisition Regulation, which provided additional guidance to federal agencies regarding the FAR overhaul.
  • Section 1826 of the NDAA for FY 2026 (Pub. L. 119-60). Section 1826(a)(6) provides nontraditional defense contractors an exemption from requirements for establishing and maintaining an acceptable purchasing system. Section 1826(b) provides the head of the contracting activity the authority to waive this exemption when in the best interest of the government.
  • Updates to the cross-reference at PGI 244.301-70(c)(3)(ii)(A) from 252.242-7005, Contractor Business Systems, to 252.242-7998, Contractor Business Systems (DEVIATION 2026-O0050).
  • Revisions to section and paragraph organization and numbering; consolidation of clause prescriptions; revisions to cited statutes; and other revisions to streamline and simplify text.

This class deviation remains in effect until rescinded or incorporated into the FAR, DFARS, and DFARS PGI. Address inquiries regarding this class deviation to osd.pentagon.ousd-a-s.mbx.dfars@mail.mil.
