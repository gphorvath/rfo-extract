PGI 217.7405 Reports.

(a) The UCA Tool module in the PARROT application of Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment (PIEE) is the Department’s repository of UCAs and UCOs.

(b) In the PIEE UCA Tool—

(1) Enter data for each UCA and each UCO, as required;

(2) Update UCAs and UCOs with any status updates (e.g., receipt of qualifying proposal, start of negotiations, or definitization) or other changes since the last reporting period;

(3) Attach a copy of the record of weighted guidelines for each definitized UCA, as required, or if there is no record of weighted guidelines (e.g., not required for a cost plus award fee contract per profit requirements in DFARS section 215.404), attach required alternative documentation; and

(4) Reconcile the data entered with SAM.gov contract data to ensure accuracy and completeness.

(c) PIEE UCA Tool guidance is in the Undefinitized Contract Action and Unpriced Change Order Reporting Guide at https://dodprocurementtoolbox.com/uploads/UCA_UCO_Reporting_Guide_a5075b4511.pdf (requires CAC access).

(d) If a discrepancy within the PIEE UCA Tool cannot be resolved, submit a detailed description of the discrepancy and actions taken to resolve within 15 days after the report finalization deadline to osd.pentagon.ousd-a-s.mbx.asda-dp-c-contractpolicy@mail.mil.

(e) Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy (DPCAP) will collect the information from the PIEE UCA Tool within 30 days after October 31 and April 30 of each year. The PIEE UCA Tool information and timely discrepancy submissions (see paragraph (d) of this section), constitute the formal Consolidated UCA Management Report for the department or agency.
