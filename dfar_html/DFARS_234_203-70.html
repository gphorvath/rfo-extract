234.203-70 Solicitation provision and contract clause.

Except for contracts subject to the exemptions at 234.201-70 paragraph (j), for cost or incentive contracts valued at $50 million or more, and for other contracts for which EVMS will be applied in accordance with 234.201-70(a)(3) and (4)—

(a) Insert the provision at 252.234-7998, Notice of Earned Value Management System, in the solicitation; and

(b) Insert the clause at 252.234-7999, Earned Value Management System, instead of the clause at FAR 52.234-4, Earned Value Management System, in the solicitation and contract.
