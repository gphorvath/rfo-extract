206.104-70 Justification content.

(a)(1) Except as provided in paragraph (b) of this section, each justification required under 206.103 will include the information at FAR 6.104-1(a).

(2) Include the information required by PGI 206.104-70 in justifications citing the authority at 206.103-170 or FAR 6.103-1.

(b) In lieu of the threshold at FAR 6.104-1(b), each justification for a sole-source 8(a) contract exceeding $100 million will include the information at FAR 6.104-1(b).
