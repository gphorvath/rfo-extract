270.100 Scope of subpart.

(a) This subpart implements a pilot program authorized by section 874 of the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2022 (Pub. L. 117-81), to use noncompetitive procedures for certain follow-on contracts to qualified businesses (see 270.101).

(b) The authority to award contracts under this subpart expires on December 27, 2029 (section 872 of the NDAA for FY 2024, Pub. L. 118-31).
