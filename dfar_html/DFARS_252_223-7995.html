252.223-7995 Prohibition of Procurement of Personal Protective Fire-Fighting Equipment Containing Perfluoroalkyl Substances or Polyfluoroalkyl Substances.

As prescribed in 223.7404(c), use the following clause: PROHIBITION OF PROCUREMENT OF PERSONAL PROTECTIVE FIRE-FIGHTING EQUIPMENT CONTAINING PERFLUOROALKYL SUBSTANCES OR POLYFLUOROALKYL SUBSTANCES (AUG 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0018)

(a) Definitions. As used in this clause—

Perfluoroalkyl substance means a man-made chemical of which all of the carbon atoms are fully fluorinated carbon atoms.

Polyfluoroalkyl substance means a man-made chemical containing at least one fully fluorinated carbon atom and at least one non-fully fluorinated carbon atom.

(b) Prohibition. The Contractor must not provide or use under this contract any personal protective fire-fighting equipment that does not must meet the specifications set forth in the most recently published edition of the National Fire Protection Associate 1970 standard.

(c) Subcontracts. The Contractor must include the substance of this clause, including this paragraph (c), in all subcontracts, including subcontracts for commercial products or commercial services, relating to covered personal protective fire-fighting equipment.

(End of clause)
