252.230-7994 Alternate A, Proposal Disclosure—Cost Accounting Practice Changes. (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

As prescribed at 230.204(b), use the following provision: ALTERNATE A, PROPOSAL DISCLOSURE—COST ACCOUNTING PRACTICE CHANGES (JUL 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

(a) The Offeror must check "yes" if the contract award will result in a required or unilateral change in cost accounting practice, including unilateral changes requested to be desirable changes: □ Yes □ No

(b) If the Offeror checked "Yes" in paragraph (a) of this provision, the Offeror must—

(1) Prepare the price proposal in response to the solicitation using the changed practice for the period of performance for which the practice will be used; and

(2) Submit a description of the changed cost accounting practice to the Contracting Officer and the Cognizant Federal Agency Official as pricing support for the proposal.

(End of provision)
