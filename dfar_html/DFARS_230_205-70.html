230.205-70 Contract clauses.

(a) Insert the clause at FAR 52.230-2, Cost Accounting Standards, with 252.230-7995, Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards, in negotiated contracts with business units that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts unless—

(1) The contract is exempted (see 48 CFR 9903.201-1);

(2) The contract is subject to modified coverage (see 230.201-70(a)(2)); or

(3) The clause prescribed in paragraph (c) or (e) of this section is used.

(b) Insert the clause at FAR 52.230-3, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, with 252.230-7996, Alternate A, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, in negotiated contracts with business units that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts when—

(1) The contract amount meets or exceeds the threshold at 230.201-70(b) but is less than the threshold at 230.201-70(a)(1); and

(2) The offeror certifies it is eligible for and elects to use modified CAS coverage (see 230.201-70(a)(2)), unless the clause prescribed in paragraph (c) or (e) of this section is used.

(c) Insert the clause at FAR 52.230-4, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices—Foreign Concerns, with the clause at 252.230-7997, Alternate A, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices—Foreign Concerns, in negotiated contracts with foreign concerns that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts, unless the contract is otherwise exempt from CAS (see 48 CFR 9903.201-1). Foreign concerns do not include foreign governments or their agents or instrumentalities.

(d) Insert the clause at FAR 52.230-6, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards, with the clause at 252.230-7998, Alternate A, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards, in contracts containing any of the clauses prescribed in paragraphs (a), (b), (c), or (e) of this section.

(e) Insert the clause at FAR 52.230-5, Cost Accounting Standards—Educational Institutions, with the clause at 252.230-7999, Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards—Educational Institutions, in negotiated contracts awarded to educational institutions that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts, unless—

(1) The contract is exempted (see 48 CFR 9903.201-1);

(2) The contract is to be performed by an FFRDC (see 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(5), using the thresholds at 230.201-70(a)(1) or (a)(2) for full or modified CAS coverage, respectively); or

(3) The provision at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(c)(6) applies, using the thresholds at 230.201-70(a)(1) or (a)(2) for full or modified CAS coverage, respectively.
