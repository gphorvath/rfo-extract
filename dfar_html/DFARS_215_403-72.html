215.403-72 Certified cost or pricing data under the Program to Accelerate Contracting and Pricing Processes.

(a) Under the program authorized by 10 U.S.C. 3709, informally known as “TINA Lite,” offerors, contractors, or subcontractors provide a subset of certified cost or pricing data required by the Truthful Cost or Pricing Data Act (formerly the Truth in Negotiations Act (TINA)). The head of the contracting activity may approve, without power of delegation, contract actions for participation in the program subject to the following:

(1) The solicitation will result in a contract action exceeding $50 million.

(2) The procurement is for requirements of a recurring nature where there is reliable, actual cost data.

(3) Contractors should have approved business systems.

(4) Price reasonableness is determined primarily using actual cost and pricing data for DoD purchases of the same or similar products.

(b) For contract actions approved to participate in the program authorized by 10 U.S.C. 3709—

(1) Use the TINA Lite Policy and Implementation Guide, to establish the extent, structure, and level of detail that the offeror or subcontractor is required to submit and certify to support a price reasonableness determination (see PGI 215.403-72);

(2) Use the Certificate of Current Cost or Pricing Data at 252.215-7988 or 252.215-7989, as applicable, in lieu of the Certificate at FAR 15.403-4; and

(3) See PGI 215.403-72(b)(3) for notification requirements.
