PGI 217.7404-1 Authorization.

(a) The requiring activity, in coordination with the contracting office, prepares the request for approval package for an undefinitized contract action (UCA) requirement. The approval package must—

(1) Document why a UCA is required (for letter contracts see DFARS 216.603);

(2) Provide a detailed explanation for the need to begin performance before definitization;

(3) Address the adverse impact on agency requirements that would result from delays for purposes of determining contractual terms, specifications, and price before beginning performance (10 U.S.C. 3371);

(4) Identify the risk of using a UCA and how the Government will mitigate such risk;

(5) Identify and justify the specific contractual instrument to be used;

(6) Establish limitations on the obligation of funds; and

(7) Provide the definitization schedule of agreed-upon events that support timely definitization.

(b) When including requirements for non-urgent spare parts and support equipment in a UCA, in addition to the requirements in paragraph (a) of this section, the request should show that inclusion of the non-urgent items is consistent with good business practices and in the best interest of the United States.

(c) When modifying the scope of a UCA after performance has already begun, in addition to the requirements in paragraph (a) of this section, the request must demonstrate that the modification is consistent with good business practices and in the best interests of the United States.

(d) A sample format with certain variations for letter contracts is provided at http://www.acq.osd.mil/dpap/dars/pgi/docs/Template_for_UCA_Authorization_Requests.pdf.
