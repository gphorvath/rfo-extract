219.106-70 Status as a SDVOSB concern.

(a) In lieu of the procedures at FAR 19.106-1(b), for a Service-Disabled Veteran Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) concern that seeks an SDVOSB set-aside or sole-source contract, verify that the offeror is designated as an SDVOSB concern certified by SBA in the Small Business Search (SBS) at https://search.certifications.sba.gov/.

(b) In lieu of the procedures at FAR 19.106-1(c)(2), a joint venture may be considered an SDVOSB concern eligible under the SDVOSB Program if the managing SDVOSB joint venture partner is designated in SBS as an SDVOSB concern certified by SBA.
