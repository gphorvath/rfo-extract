206.001-70 Applicability.

This part does not apply to—

(a) As authorized by 10 U.S.C. 1091, personal services contracts awarded for health care to individuals using the procedures at 237.201-70(b)(1);

(b) As authorized by 10 U.S.C. 4022 for prototype projects, follow-on production contracts for products developed pursuant to the “other transactions” authority, when the contracting officer receives sufficient documentation from the agreements officer issuing the other transaction agreement for the prototype project that—

(1) The other transaction agreement included provisions for a follow-on production contract (10 U.S.C. 4022(f)(1)); and

(2) Where applicable, the threshold at 10 U.S.C. 4022(a)(2)(C) and the requirements at 10 U.S.C. 4022(f)(2)(A) and (B) have been met. See PGI 206.001-70 for additional guidance.
