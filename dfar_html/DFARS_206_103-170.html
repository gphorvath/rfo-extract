206.103-170 Only one responsible source and no other supplies or services will satisfy agency requirements.

(a) Authority. See FAR 6.103-1. Follow the procedures at PGI 206.103-170 prior to soliciting a proposal without providing for full and open competition under this authority.

(b) Unsolicited proposals for studies, analyses, or consulting services. Section 8059 of the Defense Appropriations Act for Fiscal Year 1991 (Pub. L. 101-511) and similar sections in subsequent defense appropriations acts prohibit departments and agencies from contracting for studies, analyses, or consulting services (see FAR subpart 37.4) based on an unsolicited proposal without providing for full and open competition, unless—

(1) The head of the contracting activity, or a designee no lower than chief of the contracting office, determines that—

(i) Following thorough technical evaluation, only one source is fully qualified to perform the proposed work;

(ii) The unsolicited proposal offers significant scientific or technological promise, represents the product of original thinking, and was submitted in confidence; or

(iii) The contract benefits the national defense by taking advantage of a unique and significant industrial accomplishment or by ensuring financial support to a new product or idea;

(2) A civilian official of the DoD, whose appointment has been confirmed by the Senate, determines the award to be in the interest of national defense; or

(3) The contract is related to improvement of equipment that is in development or production.

(c) Test articles and associated support services. This authority may be used for acquisitions of test articles and associated support services from a designated foreign source under the DoD Foreign Comparative Testing Program (10 U.S.C. 2350a(g)).

(d) Brand-name or equal descriptions. Notwithstanding FAR 6.103-1(d), in accordance with section 888(a) of the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2017 (Pub. L. 114-328), the justification and approval (J&A) addressed in FAR 6.104 is required to use brand name or equal descriptions.

(e) Proprietary specifications or standards. In accordance with section 888(a) of the NDAA for FY 2017, the J&A addressed in FAR 6.104 is required to use proprietary specifications and standards.
