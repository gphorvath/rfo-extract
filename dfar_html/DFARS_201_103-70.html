201.103-70 Purpose.

(a) The defense acquisition system, as defined in 10 U.S.C. 3001(a), exists to manage the investments of the United States in technologies, programs, and product support necessary to achieve the national security strategy prescribed by the President pursuant to section 108 of the National Security Act of 1947 (50 U.S.C. 3043) and to support the United States Armed Forces.

(b) The investment strategy of DoD must be postured to support not only the current United States Armed Forces, but also future Armed Forces of the United States.

(c) The primary purpose of the defense acquisition system is to expeditiously provide the armed forces with the capabilities necessary to operate effectively, to address evolving threats, and to sustain the military advantage of the United States in the most cost-effective manner practicable (10 U.S.C. 3102).
