237.802-70 Prohibition on contracting for firefighting or security-guard functions.

(a) Except as provided in paragraphs (b) and (c), 10 U.S.C. 2465 prohibits DoD from contracting for the performance of firefighting or security-guard functions at any military installation or facility.

(b) The prohibition in paragraph (a) does not apply to a contract—

(1) For performance at a location outside the U.S. and its outlying areas to avoid using members of the armed forces for firefighting or security-guard functions at the expense of unit readiness;

(2) For performance on a Government-owned but privately operated installation;

(3) Or renewal of a contract, for the performance of a function under contract on September 24, 1983; or

(4) That is for a period of 1 year or less and covers the performance of functions normally performed by members of the armed forces who are or will be unavailable.

(c) This prohibition does not apply to services at installations being closed (section 2907 of the NDAA for FY 1996 (Pub. L. 103-160)) (see subpart 237.74).
