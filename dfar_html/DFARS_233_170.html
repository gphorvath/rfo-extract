233.170 General.

(a) Limited information disclosure. If the Government limits information disclosure under the authority provided at 240.271-6(d), actions taken by the Government under such authority are prohibited from challenge in a bid protest before the GAO or in any Federal court (section 806 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2011 (Pub. L. 111-383)).

(b) Protested acquisitions valued at $1 billion or more. Follow the procedures at PGI 233.170(b) for protested acquisitions valued at $1 billion or more.

(c) Reporting requirement for protests of solicitations or awards. Follow the procedures at PGI 233.170(c) for reporting information on protests involving the same contract award or proposed award that have been filed at both the GAO and the U.S. Court of Federal Claims.
