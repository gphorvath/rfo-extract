206.103-470 International agreement.

(a) Authority. 10 U.S.C. 3204(a)(4).

(b) Agreements. Pursuant to 10 U.S.C. 3204(e)(4)(E), the justification and approval described in FAR 6.104 is not required if the head of the contracting activity prepares a document that describes the terms of an agreement or treaty or the written directions, such as a Letter of Offer and Acceptance, that has the effect of requiring the use of other than competitive procedures for the acquisition.
