223.7402 Prohibitions.

(a) Fire-fighting agent. (1) After October 1, 2026, do not procure fire-fighting agent that contains detectable amounts of perfluoroalkyl substances or polyfluoroalkyl substances.

(2) This prohibition does not apply to procurements of fire-fighting agent for use—

(i) Onboard ocean-going vessels, including use in pier-side inspection, testing, and maintenance;

(ii) Necessary to conduct testing to meet military specification qualification requirements and to ensure quality standards of fire-fighting-agent inventory;

(iii) In connection with the research, development, test, and evaluation of fluorine-free fire-fighting agents;

(iv) On naval nuclear submarine propulsion plants; or

(v) In any tactical vehicle, or equipment, that is incompatible with fluorine-free fire-fighting agents.

(b) Covered items. Do not procure any covered item that contains perfluorooctane sulfonate (PFOS) or perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA).

(c) Personal protective fire-fighting equipment. On or after October 1, 2026, for procurements that include personal protective fire-fighting equipment for use by firefighters, such equipment must meet the specifications set forth in the most recently published edition of the National Fire Protection Associate 1970 standard, unless the Secretary of Defense determines that such covered personal protective fire-fighting equipment is unavailable.
