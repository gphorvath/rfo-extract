223.7403 Procedures.

(a) Fire-fighting agent. After October 1, 2026, do not issue a solicitation for any fire-fighting agent that contains detectable amounts of perfluoroalkyl or polyfluoroalkyl substances, unless the requiring activity provides documentation of the exemption at 223.7402(a)(2). Maintain the documentation in the contract file.

(b) Covered items. Do not procure any covered item that contains perfluorooctane sulfonate (PFOS) or perfluorooctanoic acid (PFOA).

(c) Personal protective fire-fighting equipment. On or after October 1, 2026, do not issue a solicitation for any personal protective fire-fighting equipment for use by firefighters that does not meet the specifications set forth in the most recently published edition of the National Fire Protection Associate 1970 standard, unless the requiring activity provides documentation of the determination at 223.7402(c). Maintain the documentation in the contract file.
