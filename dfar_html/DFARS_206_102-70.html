206.102-70 Establishing or maintaining alternative sources.

(a) Authority. To establish or maintain alternative sources, departments and agencies may use this authority to totally or partially exclude a particular source from a contract action.

(b) Requirements. Identify the excluded source in the determination and findings and its supporting documentation (FAR 6.102-1(b)). Include the information at PGI 206.102-70(b), as applicable, and any other information that may be pertinent, in the supporting documentation.
