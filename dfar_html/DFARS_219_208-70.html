219.208-70 Partnership agreement.

(a) Procedures for competitive 8(a). For requirements processed under the Partnership Agreement (PA) between the SBA and DoD, follow the procedures at PGI 219.208-70(a) to request eligibility determinations.

(b) Contract negotiation. For sole-source requirements processed under the PA, follow the procedures at PGI 219.208-70(b).

(c) Pricing the 8(a) contract. For requirements processed under the PA—

(1) Obtain certified cost or pricing data from the 8(a) contractor, if required by FAR subpart 15.4; and

(2) SBA concurrence in the negotiated price is not required. However, notify the SBA prior to withdrawing a requirement from the 8(a) Program due to failure to agree on price or other terms and conditions. Such notification is not required for purchase orders not exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold.

(d) Preparing the contracts. When preparing contracts under the PA, follow the procedures at PGI 219.208-70(d).
