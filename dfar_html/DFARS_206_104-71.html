206.104-71 Approval of justification.

In lieu of the thresholds at FAR 6.104-2(b) Table 6-1 and in accordance with 10 U.S.C. 3204(e)(1)(B), approval levels are as follows:

Table 206-1. Approval authorities for other than full and open competition.

Value (including options)Approval Authority
$10 million or belowContracting officer Accomplished by certification required at FAR 6.104-1(a)(12)
Exceeding $10 million but not more than $100 millionCompetition advocate Not delegable
Exceeding $100 million but not more than $500 millionHead of the contracting activity (HCA) Delegable in accordance with paragraph (a) of this section
Exceeding $500 millionSenior procurement executive (SPE) Delegable in accordance with paragraph (b) of this section

(a) The HCA may only delegate this authority to an officer or employee who—

(1) If a member of the armed forces, is a general or flag officer; or

(2) If a civilian, is serving in a position with a grade under the General Schedule (or any other schedule for civilian officers or employees) that is comparable to or higher than the grade of brigadier general or rear admiral (lower half).

(b) When the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment) is acting as the SPE for DoD, this authority is delegable only to—

(1) An Assistant Secretary of Defense; or

(2) For a defense agency, an officer or employee serving in, assigned, or detailed to that agency who—

(i) If a member of the armed forces, is serving in a rank above brigadier general or rear admiral (lower half); or

(ii) If a civilian, is serving in a position with a grade under the General Schedule (or any other schedule for civilian officers or employees) that is comparable to or higher than the grade of major general or rear admiral.

(c) For a non-competitive follow-on acquisition to a previous award for the same supply or service supported by a justification for other than full and open competition citing the authority at FAR 6.103-1, follow the procedures at PGI 206.104-71.

(d) In accordance with section 823 of the NDAA for FY 2020 (Pub. L. 116-92), the HCA is the approval authority for a proposed sole-source 8(a) contract exceeding $100 million. This authority is delegable in accordance with paragraph (a) of this section.
