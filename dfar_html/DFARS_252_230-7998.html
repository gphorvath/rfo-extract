252.230-7998 Alternate A, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards. (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

As prescribed at 230.205-70(d), use the following provision: ALTERNATE A, ADMINISTRATION OF COST ACCOUNTING STANDARDS (JUL 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

(a) Substitute “252.230-7995, Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards” for the reference to FAR 52.230-2, Cost Accounting Standards, wherever it appears in the clause at FAR 52.230-6, Administration of Cost Accounting Standards.

(b) Substitute “252.230-7996, Alternate A, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices” for the reference to FAR 52.230-3, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices, wherever it appears in the clause at FAR 52.230-6.

(c) Substitute “252.230-7997, Alternate A, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices—Foreign Concerns” for the reference to FAR 52.230-4, Disclosure and Consistency of Cost Accounting Practices—Foreign Concerns, wherever it appears in the clause at FAR 52.230-6.

(d) Substitute “252.230-7999, Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards— Educational Institutions” for the reference to FAR 52.230-5, Cost Accounting Standards—Educational Institutions, wherever it appears in the clause at FAR 52.230-6.

(End of clause)
