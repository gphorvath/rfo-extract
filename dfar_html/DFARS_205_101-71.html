205.101-71 Exemptions.

(a) A presolicitation notice to the GPE is not required when the proposed contract action—

(1) Will be awarded and performed outside the United States and its outlying areas; and

(2) Will be solicited for only local sources.

(b) This exemption does not apply to proposed contract actions covered by the World Trade Organization Government Procurement Agreement or a Free Trade Agreement (see FAR subpart 25.4);
