230.201-70 Applicability.

(a) In accordance with section 1806(a)(2) of the NDAA for FY 2026—

(1) In lieu of FAR 30.201(b) and the guidance at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(a), full Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) coverage applies to contractor business units that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts and that—

(i) Receive a single CAS-covered contract award of $100 million or more; or

(ii) Received CAS-covered contracts during its preceding cost accounting period with a net value of $100 million or more.

(2) In lieu of FAR 30.201(b) and the guidance at 48 CFR 9903.201-2(b), modified CAS coverage applies to contractor business units that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts and that—

(i) Receive a single CAS-covered contract award of less than $100 million; or

(ii) Received CAS-covered contracts during its preceding cost accounting period with a net value of less than $100 million.

(3) If a business unit has mixed DoD and other Federal contracts or subcontracts, then FAR 30.201(b) continues to apply.

(b) In lieu of FAR 30.201(d), the threshold for determining the tentative applicability of CAS at the contract level is the amount set forth in 41 U.S.C. 1502(b)(1)(B).
