206.103-570 Authorized or required by statute.

(a) Authority. See FAR 6.103-5.

(b) Authorized by statute. Departments and agencies may use this authority to acquire the following:

(1)(i) Supplies and services from military exchange stores outside the United States for use by the armed forces outside the United States in accordance with 10 U.S.C. 2424(a) and subject to the limitations of 10 U.S.C. 2424(b).

(ii) The limitations of 10 U.S.C. 2424(b)(1) and (2) do not apply to the purchase of soft drinks manufactured in the United States. For the purposes of 10 U.S.C. 2424, soft drinks manufactured in the United States are brand name carbonated sodas, manufactured in the United States, as evidenced by product markings.

(2) Police, fire protection, airfield operation, or other community services from local governments at military installations to be closed under the circumstances in 237.7401 (section 2907 of the Defense Authorization Act of FY 1994 (Pub. L. 103-160)).

(3) Products and services under the Pilot Program to Incentivize Contracting with Employee-Owned Businesses (see subpart 270.1).

(c) Limitations.

(1) 10 U.S.C. 4141 precludes use of this exception for awards to colleges or universities for the performance of research and development, or for the construction of any research or other facility, unless—

(i) The statute authorizing or requiring award specifically—

(A) States that the statute modifies or supersedes the provisions of 10 U.S.C. 4141;

(B) Identifies the particular college or university involved; and

(C) States that award is being made in contravention of 10 U.S.C. 4141(a); and

(ii) The Secretary of Defense provides Congress written notice of intent to award. The contract cannot be awarded until 180 days have elapsed since the date Congress received the notice of intent to award. Contracting activities must submit a draft notice of intent with supporting documentation through channels to the Principal Director, Defense Pricing, Contracting, and Acquisition Policy, Office of the Under Secretary of Defense (Acquisition and Sustainment).

(2) The limitation in paragraph (c)(1) of this section applies only if the statute authorizing or requiring award was enacted after September 30, 1989.

(3) Subsequent statutes may provide different or additional constraints on the award of contracts to specified colleges and universities. Contracting officers should consult legal counsel on a case-by-case basis.

(d) 8(a) sole-source contracts. In lieu of FAR 6.104-1(b) and in accordance with section 823 of the NDAA for FY 2020 (Pub. L. 116-92), under this authority departments and agencies are authorized to award 8(a) sole-source contracts (15 U.S.C. 637(a)), for—

(1) An amount exceeding $100 million when—

(i) The contracting officer justifies the use of a sole-source contract in writing as described in FAR 6.104-1;

(ii) The justification is approved in accordance with 206.104-71; and

(iii) The justification and related information are publicly available after award in accordance with FAR 6.301; or

(2)(i) An amount not exceeding $100 million; and

(ii) In lieu of FAR 6.103-5(e), a J&A is not required.
