252.230-7999 Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards—Educational Institution. (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

As prescribed at 230.205-70(e), use the following provision: ALTERNATE A, COST ACCOUNTING STANDARDS—EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTION (JUL 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

(a) Substitute the thresholds for full and modified Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) coverage at Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) 230.201-70(a)(1) and (a)(2), respectively, for the thresholds in 48 CFR 9903.201-2 and 48 CFR 9903.202-1, wherever they appear in the clause at Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 52.230-5, Cost Accounting Standards—Educational Institution.

(b) Substitute “DFARS 230.205-70(b)(1)” for the reference to FAR 30.205(b) in paragraph (d)(2) of the clause at 52.230-5, Cost Accounting Standards—Educational Institution.

(End of clause)
