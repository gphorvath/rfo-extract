234.7101 Applicability.

(a) This subpart applies to contracts supporting any acquisition and/or sustainment program, if that program is expected to exceed $100 million in one or more of the following acquisition pathways and to meet the following program criteria, when the contract is expected to exceed either of the following amounts:

(1) $50 million.

(b) This subpart also applies to contracts supporting any other program that is expected to exceed $100 million and is determined to be high-risk or of high-technical interest by the cost and software data reporting (CSDR) plan approval authority.
