234.7105 Contract clause.

Use the basic or the alternate of the clause at 252.234-7004, Cost and Software Data Reporting, in solicitations and contracts for defense acquisition programs that are expected to exceed $100 million and meet the requirements for cost and software data reporting as follows:

(a) Use the basic clause in solicitations and contracts that exceed $50 million and that meet the criteria at 234.7101(a) and (a)(1).

(b) Use the alternate I clause in solicitations and contracts that exceed $20 million, but are less than or equal to $50 million, and that meet the criteria at 234.7101(a)(2) or (b).
