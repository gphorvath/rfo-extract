217.7404-1 Authorization.

(a) The contracting officer will obtain approval from the head of the contracting activity before—

(1) Entering into a UCA;

(2) Including requirements for non-urgent spare parts and support equipment in a UCA; or

(3) Modifying the scope of a UCA when performance has already begun.

(b) Follow PGI 217.7404-1 for approval request procedures.
