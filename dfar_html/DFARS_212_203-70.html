212.203-70 Evaluation factors.

(a) Include an evaluation factor regarding supply chain risk (see 240.271) when acquiring information technology, whether as a service or as a supply, that is a covered system, is a part of a covered system, or is in support of a covered system, as defined in 240.271-2.

(b) When acquiring transportation or transportation related services, see PGI 212.203-70.
