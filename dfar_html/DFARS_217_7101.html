217.7101 Definitions.

As used in this subpart— Master agreement for repair and alteration of vessels—

(1) Means a written instrument of understanding, negotiated between a contracting activity and a contractor that—

(i) Contains contract clauses, terms, and conditions applying to future contracts for repairs, alterations, and/or additions to vessels; and

(ii) Contemplates separate future contracts that will incorporate by reference or attachment the required and applicable clauses agreed upon in the master agreement; and

(2) Is not a contract.

Job order means a fixed price contract incorporating, by reference or attachment, a master agreement for repair and alteration of vessels, that—

(1) May include clauses pertaining to subjects not covered by the master agreement; but applicable to the job order being awarded; and

(2) Applies to a specific acquisition and sets forth the scope of work, price, delivery date, and other appropriate terms that apply to the particular job order.
