231.205-6 Compensation for personal services.

(e) Bonuses or other payments in excess of normal salaries paid to contractor employees related to restructuring costs after a business combination are unallowable on DoD contracts funded by fiscal year 1996 appropriations or later in accordance with section 8122 of the Department of Defense Appropriations Act, 1996 (Pub. L. 104-61), and similar sections in subsequent DoD appropriations acts. See 231.205-70 for definitions of business combination and restructuring costs.

(l) Fringe benefit costs that violate laws, employer-employee agreements, or contractor established policy are unallowable.
