217.203-70 Contract clauses.

(a)(1) Insert the clause at 252.217-7000, Exercise of Option to Fulfill Foreign Military Sales Commitments, in solicitations and contracts, when an option may be used for foreign military sales and the foreign military sales country is known.

(2) Insert the clause with its alternate I in solicitations and contracts, when an option may be used for foreign military sales and the foreign military sale country is not known.

(3) Do not use the clause or its alternate in contracts for establishment or replenishment of DoD inventories or stocks, or acquisitions made under DoD cooperative logistics support arrangements.

(b) Insert the clause at 252.217-7001, Surge Option, in solicitations and contracts, when a surge option is needed in support of industrial capability production planning.

(1) Insert the percentage or quantity of increase the option represents in paragraph (a) of the clause to ensure adequate quantities are available to meet item requirements.

(2) Change 30 days in paragraphs (b)(2) and (d)(1) to longer periods, if appropriate.

(3) Change the 24-month period in paragraph (c)(3), if appropriate.
