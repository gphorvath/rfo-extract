252.230-7993 Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards Notices and Certification. (DEVIATION 2026-O0006)

As prescribed at 230.204(a), use the following provision: ALTERNATE A, COST ACCOUNTING STANDARDS NOTICES AND CERTIFICATION (JUL 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0006) The following applies to business units subject to Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts (see Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement (DFARS) 230.201-70 and 48 CFR 9903.201):

(a) Substitute the thresholds for full and modified CAS coverage at DFARS 230.201-70(a)(1) and (a)(2), respectively, for the thresholds in 48 CFR 9903.201-2 and 48 CFR 9903.202-1, wherever they appear in the provision at Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR) 52.230-1, Cost Accounting Standards Notices and Certification.

(b) Substitute $100 million for $50 million wherever it appears in the provision at FAR 52.230-1.

(c) Substitute “DFARS 230.205-70(b)(1)” for the reference to FAR 30.205(b) wherever it appears in the provision at FAR 52.230-1.

(End of provision)
