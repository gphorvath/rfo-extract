230.204 Solicitation provisions.

(a) Insert the provision at FAR 52.230-1, Cost Accounting Standards Notices and Certification, with 252.230-7993, Alternate A, Cost Accounting Standards Notices and Certification, in solicitations for contracts to business units subject to CAS that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts (see 230.201-70 and 48 CFR 9903.201).

(b) Insert the provision at 252.230-7994, Proposal Disclosure—Cost Accounting Practice Changes, in lieu of the provision at FAR 52.230-7, Proposal Disclosure— Cost Accounting Practice Changes, in solicitations for contracts to business units subject to CAS that have only DoD contracts and subcontracts (see 230.201-70 and 48 CFR 9903.201).
