217.500 Scope of subpart.

(a) Unless more specific statutory authority exists, the procedures in FAR subpart 17.5 and this subpart apply to all purchases, except micro-purchases, made for DoD by another agency. This includes orders under a task or delivery order contract entered into by the other agency (section 814 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 1999 (Pub. L. 105-261)).

(b) A contracting activity from one DoD component may provide acquisition assistance to deployed DoD units or personnel from another DoD component. See PGI 217.502-70 for guidance and procedures.
