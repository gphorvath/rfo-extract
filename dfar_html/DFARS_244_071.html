244.071 Contractor purchasing system exemption.

(a) Nontraditional defense contractors are exempt from the requirements to establish and maintain an acceptable purchasing system (section 1826(a)(6) of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2026 (Pub. L. 119-60)).

(b) The head of the contracting activity (HCA) may waive the exemption in paragraph (a) of this section by written determination that applying the requirement for an acceptable purchasing system for nontraditional defense contractors is in the best interest of the DoD. The HCA may delegate this authority no lower than the chief of the contracting office.
