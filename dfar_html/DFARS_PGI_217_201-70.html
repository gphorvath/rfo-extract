PGI 217.201-70 Use of options.

(a) Options may be used for foreign military sales requirements.

(b) Consider use of surge options to support industrial capability. A surge option allows the Government, prior to final delivery, to—

(1) Accelerate the contractor's production rate in accordance with a surge production plan or a delivery schedule provided by the contractor under the terms of the contract; and

(2) Purchase additional quantities of supplies or services.

(c) See DFARS subpart 217.74 for limitations on the use of undefinitized options.
