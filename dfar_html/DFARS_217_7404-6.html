217.7404-6 Allowable profit.

(a) Negotiation.

(1) When negotiating the final price of a UCA after a substantial portion of the required performance is complete, the head of the contracting activity will ensure the profit allowed reflects the following:

(i) Reduced risk on work already completed. This is reduced cost risk to the contractor for costs incurred during contract performance before negotiation of the final price.

(ii) Reduced risk on remaining work. This is reduced cost risk to the contractor for costs expected to be incurred during performance of the remainder of the contract after negotiation of the final price.

(iii) Increased risk for work completed before contract award. This is increased cost risk to the contractor for costs incurred prior to the award of the UCA when such costs—

(A) Would have been directly chargeable to the contract if incurred after the award of the contract; and

(B) Were incurred to meet an anticipated contract delivery schedule or anticipated contract price targets of the Government for acquisition of a major defense acquisition program or major system.

(iv) Increased risk from long Government delays. This is increased cost risk to the contractor when negotiations continue for more than 180 days after the date on which the contractor submitted the qualifying proposal to definitize the UCA.

(v) The profit requirements in section 215.404 (see PGI 217.7404-6).

(2) Document the risk assessment in the price negotiation memorandum.

(b) Definitization. If a contractor submits a qualifying proposal to definitize a UCA, and the contracting officer takes longer than the 180-day period to definitize it, the profit allowed must accurately reflect the cost risk of the contractor that existed on the date the contractor submitted the qualifying proposal.
