252.223-7996 Safer Choice Cleaning Products.

As prescribed in 223.471(c), insert the following clause: SAFER CHOICE CLEANING PRODUCTS (AUG 2026) (DEVIATION 2026-O0018) The Contractor must, to the maximum extent practicable, deliver cleaning products under this contract certified—

(a) Under the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Safer Choice program (https://www.epa.gov/saferchoice); or

(b) By a third-party organization that provides certification in a manner consistent with the EPA Safer Choice program.

(End of clause)
