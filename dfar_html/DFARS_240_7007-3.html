240.7007-3 Solicitation provision and contract clause.

(a) Insert the provision at 252.240-7998, Prohibition on the Procurement of Covered Semiconductor Products and Services from Companies Providing Covered Semiconductor Products and Services to Huawei—Representation, in solicitations that include the clause at 252.240-7999.

(b) Insert the clause at 252.240-7999, Prohibition on the Procurement of Covered Semiconductor Products and Services from Companies Providing Covered Semiconductor Products and Services to Huawei, in solicitations and contracts for the procurement of any covered semiconductor products and services, including solicitations and contracts for commercial products or commercial services, unless waived.
