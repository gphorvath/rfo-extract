217.7405 Reports.

(a) To provide enhanced management and oversight, departments and agencies must—

(1) Report UCAs and UCOs with a value exceeding $5 million, and any subsequent status changes, within 15 days of issue;

(2) Finalize Consolidated UCA Management Reports by October 31 and April 30 of each year; and

(3) For each definitized UCA with a value of $100 million or more, submit—

(i) A copy of the record of weighted guidelines; or

(ii) If a record of weighted guidelines is not required, alternative documentation addressing appropriate recognition of the contractor’s reduced cost risk during the undefinitized performance period.

(b) Follow the procedures at PGI 217.7405 for required reporting and documentation.
