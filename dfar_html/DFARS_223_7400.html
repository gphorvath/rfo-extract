223.7400 Scope of subpart.

This subpart implements the following:

(a) Section 322(b), (c), and (d) of the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) for Fiscal Year (FY) 2020 (Pub. L. 116-92).

(b) Section 333 of the National Defense Authorization Act for Fiscal Year 2021 (Pub. L. 116-283).

(c) Section 345 of the NDAA for FY 2023 (Pub. L. 117-263).

(d) Sections 315 and 316 of the NDAA for FY 2026 (Pub. L. 119-60).
