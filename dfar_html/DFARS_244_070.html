244.070 Contract clauses.

(a) Except as provided in 244.071—

(1) Insert the clause at 252.244-7998, Contractor Purchasing System Administration—Basic, in solicitations and contracts containing the clause at FAR 52.244-2, Subcontracts; or

(2) Insert the clause at 252.244-7998, Contractor Purchasing System Administration—Alternate I, in solicitations and contracts that contain the clause at 252.246-7007, Contractor Counterfeit Electronic Part Detection and Avoidance System, but do not contain FAR 52.244-2, Subcontracts.

(b) Insert the clause at 252.244-7999, Subcontracts for Commercial Products and Commercial Services, in solicitations and contracts, including solicitations and contracts using FAR part 12 procedures for the acquisition of commercial products and commercial services.
