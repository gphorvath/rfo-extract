9.104-6 Federal Awardee Performance and Integrity Information.

 

(a)

(1) Before awarding a contract exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, review the responsibility/qualification (R/Q) records in the System for Award Management (SAM) at https://www.sam.gov.

(2) The R/Q records also identify—

(i) An affiliate that is an immediate owner or subsidiary of the offeror, if any; and

(ii) All predecessors of the offeror that held a Federal contract or grant within the last three years.

 

(b)

(1) When making a responsibility determination, consider all the information available in the R/Q records, as well as other past performance information on the offeror in CPARS.gov (see part 42).

(2) For evaluation of information available relating to an affiliate of the offeror, see 9.104-3(c).

(3) For source selection evaluations of past performance, see 15.305(a)(2). When determining the weight and relevance of the past performance information consider how it relates to the present acquisition.

(4) Since the R/Q records may contain information on any of the offeror's previous contracts and information covering a 5-year period, consider whether its relevant to a determination of present responsibility. For example, a prior administrative action such as debarment, suspension, voluntary exclusion, or administrative agreement, that has expired or otherwise been resolved, or information relating to contracts for completely different products or services, might be irrelevant.

(5) Because integrity records in SAM provides information about prime contractors, the contracting officer posts information about a subcontractor, such as trafficking in persons violations, to the record of the prime contractor (see 42.1503(h)(1)(v)). The prime contractor may post in SAM Workspace any mitigating factors. Consider any mitigating factors posted in SAM by the prime contractor, such as degree of compliance by the prime contractor with the terms of clause 52.222-50.

(c) Upon obtaining relevant R/Q records regarding criminal, civil, or administrative proceedings in connection with the award or performance of a Government contract; terminations for default or cause; determinations of nonresponsibility because the contractor does not have a satisfactory performance record or a satisfactory record of integrity and business ethics; or comparable information relating to a grant, unless the contractor has already been debarred, suspended, or has agreed to a voluntary exclusion—

(1) Promptly request from the offeror additional information to demonstrate the offeror's responsibility (but see 9.405); and

(2) Notify, prior to proceeding with award, in accordance with agency procedures (see 9.406-3(a) and 9.407-3(a)), the agency official responsible for initiating debarment or suspension action, if the information appears appropriate for the official's consideration.

(d) For contracts exceeding the simplified acquisition threshold, document the file to indicate how the information in the R/Q records was considered in the responsibility determination, as well as any action taken as a result. Document nonresponsibility determinations in the integrity records part of the SAM.gov Workspace in accordance with 9.105-2 (b)(2).
