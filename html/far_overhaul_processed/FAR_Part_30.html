Part 30 - Cost Accounting Standards Administration

The Cost Accounting Standards Board is currently evaluating the regulatory coverage contained in FAR Part 30 related to cost impacts, including the need for additional clarity of the Board’s definition of “increased costs in the aggregate” as required by 41 U.S.C 1503(b) and the handling of cost impacts for unilateral cost accounting practice changes. Coverage on these issues will be removed from the FAR by rulemaking at a later date and addressed instead in CAS by the Board through a separate rulemaking.
